EARLY CONCEPT · NOT A PLAYABLE GAME YET

THE ISLANDS ARE YOURS TO SHAPE

01 / THE VISION

More than a map.
A place to live.

The first version draws inspiration from Trinidad and Tobago: island atmosphere, local everyday life, familiar streets and neighborhoods, and systems that reward players for building a life.

The ambition is big, but the first prototype should be small: one area, one character, one interaction, and one meaningful activity.

02 / CORE PILLARS

A world built around your choices.

01

Build your life

Find work, earn money, manage expenses, and work toward a home.

02

Move your way

Explore neighborhoods and make travel part of your story.

03

Know your people

Build friendships, reputation, and community ties.

04

Make it yours

Customize your character, space, and lifestyle.

These are design goals, not completed game features.

03 / DEVELOPMENT

From idea to playable proof.

  1. Design foundation

    Core loop, target devices, scope, references, and design document.

  2. Movement prototype

    One small test area, controllable character, camera, and basic movement.

  3. First activity

    One interaction or simple job/task with a start and finish.

  4. Performance and feedback

    Test on target hardware before expanding.

This is a concept website, not a playable 3D game. Multiplayer, vehicles, jobs, and a large world remain future ideas until a working prototype demonstrates them.
